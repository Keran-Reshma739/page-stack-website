❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why doesn't the Back button duplicate stack items?

When Back (POP) is clicked, stack.pop() removes the top element. On loading the target page, stack.peek() matches the target URL, preventing redundant push() operations.

Q: How is stack state persisted across full browser refreshes?

State is serialized into JSON and stored in HTML5 localStorage.setItem('stack_page_history_v2', ...) after every mutation.
